## Job verification rule
Every job gets one of three liveness levels:
- **verified**: seen on the company's own current job portal, with a live posting or a working apply button.
- **live-recent**: posted by the company itself within the last 14 days on a job site, seen in a job-alert email or on a page {{FIRST_NAME}} opened, and not found on the company's own portal.
- **unverified**: everything else. This includes a PDF on a server, a university job PDF older than about six weeks, an aggregator page alone, and any page that says the posting is closed.

For every suggestion give the level, the link, the posting date (or "undated") and how liveness was confirmed. Write no cover letter for an unverified job.
