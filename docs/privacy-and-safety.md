# Privacy and safety

## What Claude does and does not do in this kit
| Does | Does not |
|---|---|
| Reads public career pages and university job pages | Sign in to any site |
| Reads job-alert emails you paste into the chat | Search, scroll or collect postings on LinkedIn or other sign-in sites |
| Reads one posting page that you opened and shared | Use scrapers, private APIs or bulk downloads |
| Writes drafts, files and tracker entries | Apply, submit forms, send emails or messages, make calls |
| Asks when a fact about you is missing | Invent or "improve" facts about you |

## Why no LinkedIn automation
Job sites with accounts set their own terms of use, and automated collection can get an account restricted. Job alerts give you the same postings by email, with no risk. If a page shows a sign-in wall or a CAPTCHA, the skills stop there and tell you.

## Your data
- Your CV, certificates and tracker stay in your own Claude Project. Nothing in this kit sends them anywhere else.
- Do not commit your own documents or tracker rows to a public fork. The `.gitignore` blocks `.docx`, `.pdf` and folders named `my-*` or `private`, but check before you push.
- Never put your passport number, residence-permit number, tax ID, bank details or health information into the project instructions or the tracker. No part of the kit needs them.
- Contact persons in your tracker are real people. Keep only what you need to follow up (name, organisation, work email or phone), and delete it when the search ends.

## Your employer's data
If you already work somewhere, describe your work at the level your reference letter does. Do not paste internal documents, code, customer names or product details into the project. Add a line to the truth rules if your employer has confidentiality limits.

## Honesty
The truth rules protect you. A claim that fails in an interview costs more than a missing keyword. The skills keep a claim-by-claim source table for every letter for this reason.
