# Changelog

## v0.1.1 (20 November 2026)
- Smoke-test fixes: example tracker rows are clearly marked as demo data that you remove yourself; job-followup labels them and leaves them out of the weekly numbers.
- First-hour guide: says what the example row shows on "what's due?" and what to do when a career page cannot be read.
- job-scan: explicit handling of career pages that load jobs with JavaScript or block reading; the weekly source rotation now goes by weekday for manual runs too.
- Project instructions template: no literal placeholder in the intro line; the two internship placeholders have separate names; clearer hints for semester-break hours and on-site days.
- Germany file: re-checked 20 November 2026; adds the 2027 minimum wage (€14.60) and Minijob limit (€633) with a Federal Government source.
- New: `scripts/check_kit.py` (structural checks), `scripts/build_skill_zips.py`, a GitHub Actions check, issue templates.

- Skills: job-scan, application-pack, job-followup, interview-prep.
